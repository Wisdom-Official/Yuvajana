Event guidelines

  1. 1 Register online in advance — you can add multiple youth delegates from your unit in a single form.
  2. 2 You will receive your Yuvajana Sammelanam Delegate ID immediately after registering.
  3. 3 Event updates and venue pin will reach you on WhatsApp within 24 hours.
  4. 4 Show your Delegate ID (QR pass) at the on-boarding counter at Ulliyeri before 4:00 PM.
