About the Conference

Awakening Purpose, Faith & Responsibility

Organised by Wisdom Youth for the Kozhikode North District, Yuvajana Sammelanam is a transformative gathering dedicated to nurturing a generation grounded in authentic Islamic faith, sound knowledge, noble character, and constructive social responsibility. Bringing together young minds across the district in Ulliyeri, the conference inspires purposeful living and positive service to humanity.

Faith & Values

Strengthening spiritual conviction, sincerity, and timeless moral values rooted in the Qur'an and Sunnah.

Knowledge & Awareness

Cultivating intellectual clarity, critical discernment, and lifelong learning to navigate modern challenges.

Youth Empowerment

Equipping youth with discipline, vision, and ethical leadership to excel in education, family, and society.

Community Service

Channeling youthful energy into compassionate humanitarian outreach, social harmony, and civic responsibility.
